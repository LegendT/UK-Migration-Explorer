# Marketing: who, how and when

**What this document is for.** The research into who should be told about this site, how, and when,
and the action plan that follows from it. It was created on 12 August 2026 as the container the
decision named, and it is empty of research until that work runs.

**It holds reasoning, never state.** Which parts are done, and what is outstanding, is
`docs/BACKLOG.md` and specifically The order's item 31, which also records the four calls that
decided the shape of this document. `scripts/validate-content.mjs` refuses work state in any planning
document but the backlog, and this one is written to that rule rather than merely complying with it:
a second list has to be kept true in two places, and this project has watched two diverge in a day.

## What constrains anything written here

- **Every channel is unpaid.** `/about/` states, on a live page, that there is no grant, no
  advertising, no sponsorship, no commercial relationship with any organisation working in this
  field, and no payment from anyone with an interest in how migration statistics are reported. A plan
  proposing paid promotion would contradict a page the site already publishes. Read that page before
  drafting, not after.
- **A named target needs a source, on the same terms as a figure.** An outlet, a journalist, a
  newsletter, a briefing or an organisation named here carries a fetched URL and a reason. Anything
  unverifiable is marked unverified and left out rather than softened, which is the rule
  `docs/HANDOFF.md` records for research of any kind in this repository.
- **The audience is inferred and has not been asked.** `docs/foundation.md` names five audiences and
  says in terms that none of them has been approached; the backlog's item 19 is that conversation and
  it has not happened. Anything here that rests on who the readers are says so where it does.
- **Success is not yet defined.** `docs/foundation.md` section 4.2 offers three candidate measures
  and none is adopted. This document recommends which to adopt and why; adopting one is the owner's
  and is item 23.
- **The site's own voice applies.** `content/style-guide.md` is published, and it binds anything
  written in the site's name: precision about what a figure shows, no attribution of claims to named
  people, and no overclaiming in either direction.
